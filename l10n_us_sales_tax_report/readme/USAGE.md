1.  Go to *Accounting → Configuration → US Sales Tax → Returns*.
2.  Create a return, pick the **State** and a **Period** (Monthly /
    Quarterly / Annual sets the dates; adjust as needed).
3.  Press **Generate**. The return collects every posted customer
    invoice and credit note in the period whose tax was booked by the US
    Sales Tax Engine for that state, and lists the taxable base and tax
    collected per jurisdiction level.
4.  Use the **Invoices** stat button to drill into the source documents.
5.  Press **Export Worksheet** for a CSV of the breakdown to transcribe
    onto the state portal / Streamlined Sales Tax return.
6.  Once submitted to the state, press **Mark Filed** to lock the
    return.

> [!NOTE]
> *Tax collected* is summed across every jurisdiction line because the
> full amount is remitted. *Taxable sales* is counted once (the
> state-level base), since the same sale is taxed by state, county and
> city simultaneously and summing the lines would multiply it.
