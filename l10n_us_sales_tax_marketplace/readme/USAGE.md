1.  Configure facilitators under *Accounting → Configuration → US Sales
    Tax → Marketplace Facilitators* (Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Walmart are
    seeded).
2.  On a sale order or customer invoice, set the **Marketplace
    Facilitator** field (manually, or from your marketplace connector).
3.  On confirm/post, the engine records source *Marketplace Facilitator
    Collected* and charges no US sales tax.
4.  On the US tax return, **Marketplace Sales** shows the
    facilitator-collected portion for the state and period.
